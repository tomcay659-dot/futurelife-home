Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

1. Who we are

FutureLife Home is a kitchen, home organization, and storage content brand serving consumers and business partners in global markets. This Privacy Policy explains how FutureLife Home handles information through this website and through social platform integrations used to manage our own brand accounts.

2. Information we may process

When we connect our own Pinterest business account or another authorized social account, we may process:

  • Account identifiers, profile name, and authorization status.
  • Boards, Pins, media files, captions, links, and publishing schedules that we create or manage.
  • Basic content performance data made available by the platform, such as impressions, saves, clicks, and engagement totals.
  • OAuth access credentials required to maintain an authorized connection. These credentials are not displayed publicly.
  • Messages you voluntarily send to us through WhatsApp or another clearly identified contact channel.

This public website does not include an account registration system, advertising tracker, or inquiry form.

3. How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate our own accounts, create and schedule original content, upload media, organize Pins into boards, monitor publishing status, retrieve basic performance information, improve our content planning, protect our systems, and respond to direct business inquiries.

4. Legal basis and consent

We process platform data through account-owner authorization, for our legitimate interest in operating and improving FutureLife Home, and where necessary to perform steps requested in a business inquiry. You may withdraw an account authorization through the relevant platform or request deletion using the process below.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the website and publishing workflow, including GitHub Pages for website hosting, Pinterest and other social platforms for account and content services, and AitoEarn for authorized social publishing infrastructure. Providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

6. International processing

Our audience, platforms, and service providers operate internationally. Information may therefore be processed in countries other than your own, subject to applicable legal safeguards and the terms of the relevant platform.

7. Retention and security

Authorization credentials are retained only while an account remains connected or while needed to provide the requested publishing function. Publishing records and performance data may be retained for up to 24 months for operational review, unless a longer period is required by law. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to limit access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and deletion

You may disconnect FutureLife Home from Pinterest or another platform through that platform's account settings. You may also request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with a direct inquiry or authorized account. See our Data Deletion Instructions. Verified deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days unless retention is legally required.

9. Children

FutureLife Home is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, platforms, or legal obligations change. The current version and effective date will remain available on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or verified data requests, contact FutureLife Home through WhatsApp at +86 137 2789 3483. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or highly sensitive information through WhatsApp.
