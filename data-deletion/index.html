Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Fastest option: disconnect FutureLife Home from the relevant social platform's account or app settings. This revokes future access through that authorization.

Request deletion from FutureLife Home

Send a WhatsApp message to +86 137 2789 3483 with the words "FutureLife Home data deletion request." Include:

  • The platform involved, such as Pinterest.
  • The public account name or account identifier needed to locate the record.
  • A description of the information you want deleted.
  • A safe way for us to confirm completion.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment details, identity documents, or other highly sensitive information.

Verification and completion

We may ask for limited verification to avoid deleting the wrong account's information. After verification, we will delete or anonymize information under our control, revoke or remove stored authorization credentials where applicable, and confirm completion. We normally complete valid requests within 30 days. Information may be retained when required by law, needed for security, or already irreversibly aggregated.

Platform copies

Deleting our internal records does not automatically remove Pins, messages, marketplace records, or other information held independently by Pinterest or another platform. Use that platform's deletion and account controls for information it holds.
